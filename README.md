# **Mamma Mia! (2008)**

## **Overview**
*Mamma Mia!* is a musical romantic comedy film directed by **Phyllida Lloyd**, based on the songs of **ABBA**. It stars **Meryl Streep**, **Amanda Seyfried**, **Pierce Brosnan**, and more. Set on a picturesque Greek island, it tells the story of a young bride-to-be trying to discover the identity of her father.

---

## **Main Characters**
- **Donna Sheridan** – Independent hotel owner and former singer
- **Sophie Sheridan** – Donna’s daughter, getting married
- **Sam, Bill, and Harry** – Sophie’s three potential fathers
- **Tanya and Rosie** – Donna’s best friends and bandmates
